## About Me

Hi, I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I am interested in understanding how the spatial organization and dynamics of molecules give rise to cellular function.

My research lies at the interface of physics and biology. By combining super-resolution fluorescence microscopy, single-particle tracking, and quantitative computational modeling, I investigate how DNA loci, RNA molecules, and protein complexes are organized and move inside living bacterial cells. By studying these molecular dynamics at high spatial and temporal resolution, I seek to uncover the physical principles that govern genome organization and gene regulation.

You can learn more about my research on the [Kim Lab Website](https://www.sjkimlab.org/).

## Research Interests
